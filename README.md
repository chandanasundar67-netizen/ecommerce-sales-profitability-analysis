# E-Commerce Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce transaction data to identify sales and profitability patterns across product categories, regions, customer segments, payment methods, and order statuses.

The goal is to transform raw transaction data into meaningful business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability performance
- Identify high-performing product categories
- Compare regional sales and profit performance
- Analyze customer segment contribution
- Evaluate payment method performance
- Examine completed, returned, and cancelled orders
- Understand factors affecting profitability
- Develop actionable business recommendations

## Dataset

The dataset contains 5,000 e-commerce transactions with information related to:

- Order details
- Customer information
- Product categories
- Regions and cities
- Customer segments
- Quantity and pricing
- Discounts
- Sales and profit
- Shipping costs
- Payment methods
- Order status
- Customer age
- Review ratings

## Key Analysis

### Category Performance

Electronics was identified as the strongest-performing category in terms of both sales and profit.

### Regional Performance

The West region recorded the highest overall sales and profit performance among the analyzed regions.

### Customer Segments

The Consumer segment contributed the highest sales, profit, and number of orders.

### Payment Methods

Digital payment methods, particularly UPI and Credit Card, represented important contributors to overall sales performance.

### Order Status

Completed orders accounted for the majority of sales and profit, while returned and cancelled orders represented a smaller portion of overall transactions.

### Discount Analysis

The analysis indicates that higher discount levels can significantly reduce profitability. High-discount transactions may result in substantially lower or negative profit.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize inventory and marketing efforts toward high-performing product categories.
- Develop targeted strategies for the Consumer customer segment.
- Investigate lower-performing regions and categories for improvement opportunities.
- Monitor discount levels to protect profit margins.
- Analyze cancelled and returned orders to identify potential operational issues.
- Use sales and profitability metrics together when evaluating product performance.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Python and data analysis techniques can be used to transform e-commerce transaction data into actionable business insights.

The analysis provides a foundation for improving product strategy, customer targeting, regional performance, and profitability management.
